Finding
-------
All 21 OTHER samples are safety refusals. The model encounters the appended
"The problem author thinks X*Y = Z" line (with a wrong equation) and instead
of solving the problem it triggers a guardrail and produces no work and no
final answer.

Interpretation
--------------
This is a Llama-3.1-8B-specific quirk. The "The problem author thinks ..."
pattern with an obviously-wrong equation apparently looks adversarial enough
to the model's safety tuning that ~1-in-5 Sycophancy prompts get refused
outright rather than answered. This is itself a distinct failure mode worth
naming in the reviewer response: the paper's qualitative reading of
Sycophancy behavior implicitly assumes the model engages with the problem,
but for small models a non-trivial fraction of responses are just refusals
that produce no answer at all.
